# 2026-10-08 Strata v0.1.39 on the single-3060 box: mmap-on-NVMe MoE wins 3× on deep prefill, matches OptLlama on decode

**Date:** 2026-10-08 (run windows 2026-10-07 23:59–2026-10-08 05:14 UTC and gap-fill 06:15–09:07 UTC)
**Category:** Experiment / benchmark
**Hardware:** backup / inference box — Intel i3-9100 (4C/4T, DDR4-2400 2-channel ≈ 38 GB/s), single RTX 3060 12 GB, 46.8 GB host RAM, **no swap**, NVMe. Container with a **42 GiB *hard* cgroup limit** (advisory stop line 40.5 — never reached by any measured value; peak 39.1 GiB under continuous 2 s sampling in the measurement window, the overnight window's cgroup sampler was defective and its point samples peak at 35.8 GiB; no OOM).
**Model:** Qwen3.8-Flash-Next `GSQ-RCO-IQ2_XS` — the **same two shards** as the [10-05/10-07/10-08 OptLlama reports](2026-10-08-qfn-o11-attempt-4-16k-98k-3060.md), referenced in place, read-only, SHA-256 recorded. **Engine:** [Strata](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata) `v0.1.39` (commit a1641e9) — a CPU/GPU hybrid MoE engine: the ~39 GB expert GGUF is **mmap'd straight from the NVMe and never loaded into RAM**; a **4476-slot / 6.01 GiB GPU expert cache** (11.68/12 GiB VRAM at boot); the 32M-row PLE table is SSD-streamed; an 839 MiB MTP draft runs in-VRAM. The only local modification: two **installer-only** `setup.py` patches (skip re-staging the 36 GB experts when `--low-ram mmap` + a local GGUF dir is given) — the engine itself is byte-identical to the tag (SHA verified before and after the whole campaign).

**TL;DR**

- **16K decode, three arms (4 scored reps on stock):** stock config **median 10.7** (12.6/11.6/8.0/9.8) ≈ *parity* with the OptLlama best (10.67); the **16 GiB RAM-budget arm 17.4** (done-lines to 21.8); no-GPU-cache arm 9.3. Against the pre-registered disjunction, the *stock* config's bottleneck is decode (10.7 < 15), not prefill (315–350 t/s clears the >300 band); the budget arm clears 15, so **16 GB of cache-residency RAM is the ~1.6× lever on this box** — the same "pin budget" axis the OptLlama 10-06 correction identified.
- **98K cold prefill: 283 s (347 t/s) and a second diverse 90K cold at 256 s (352 t/s)** — **≈3× faster than OptLlama's 832–892 s (110–118 t/s)** for the same prompt class, with 154–159 GB of NVMe reads per cold run and anon memory bounded 2.3–3.2 GB (the model is *not* being loaded into RAM; the reads are the OS file cache doing its job). Warm re-runs: 22.3/25.2 s on 98K (770–870 t/s, PLE resident in the OS page cache) and 138 s on 90.2K (654 t/s); after a server restart the “warm” cells re-fault the PLE and run at 255–308 s — the cold regime. **This engine's deep-prefill wall is not the mmap page-fault pathology** that makes the 65.9 GiB OptLlama build I/O-dominated on the same box — the PLE table is streamed, not faulted.
- **98K decode (warm, 98K ctx): 14.1 / 17.3 t/s** (2 of 3 scored) vs OptLlama 7.53 (MTP) / 6.36 (no-MTP) in the same window → **1.9–2.3×**.
- **MTP is hard-required in their serve mode** (off-MTP serve refuses to start). The off arm via their CLI tuning mode (`strata generate`, 16 tokens, single stream): 3.30/3.71/3.89 t/s → on/off ≈ **2.9× on this box**, above their published 1.6–1.8× on 6–12 core machines. Draft acceptance 0.84.
- **`--calibrate` (their 5–10 min auto-tune) ran to completion and changed nothing here**: it resolves `--pcie-frac 0.75, --spec-min-p 0.70` (claims 10.8 t/s); post-calibrate measured median 9.79 vs pre-calibrate 10.7 — on a 4-core box the sweep has no headroom.
- **Safety:** one production stop/restore pair (stop 06:15:18Z, restore 09:06:47Z, live-verified, unit file untouched); cgroup 39.1 GiB peak (continuous sampling, measurement window) / 35.8 GiB (overnight point samples, defective continuous sampler) — both under the 40.5 advisory stop line; no OOM, no swap; 39 GB of expert reads per cold 98K window is storage bandwidth, not RAM — the 42 GiB guard was never the binding constraint for *this* engine (that was the design point).

**Conclusion**

Strata's mmap-on-NVMe architecture sidesteps the exact regime that caps the 65.9 GiB OptLlama build on this box: **deep prefill ~3×, deep decode ~2×, short decode parity (stock) to 1.6× (16 GiB RAM budget)** — on 4 cores, where the budget arm's 17.4 is the interesting number and the >20 "major win" band is missed by every median. It is **not a production candidate for this box** (the always-on 35B service is 6× faster), and 12 GB of VRAM bounds its expert cache hard. The durable fact: on a low-RAM, NVMe-rich, low-core machine, *streaming* the model from storage beats *faulting* it — the 10-05/10-07 OptLlama regime and this one are the same physics seen from opposite sides of the mmap boundary.
