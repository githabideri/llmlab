## Interpretation
- Both root ports are Gen3 x4 — the motherboard slots are electrically x4.
- GTX 1050 endpoint advertises x16 but is limited to x4 by the upstream root port.
- All four ports target Gen3 (LnkCtl2) but operate at Gen1 (LnkSta = 2.5 GT/s).
- The Gen1 speed is persistent under load (see pcie-link-samples.csv).
- Cause of the speed downgrade is unresolved. Possible explanations: BIOS PCIe
  speed override, DMI link training, PCH firmware policy, or signal integrity.
  No AER errors observed; PCIe equalization completed successfully.
